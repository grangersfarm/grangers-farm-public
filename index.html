Welcome to Granger’s Farm

A little closer to the land.
A little more time together.

Christmas begins with rosy cheeks, winter air, and a tree to bring home with the people you love. In autumn, there are pumpkins to pick, our famous corn maze to wander, and baby goats to hold close. Here at Granger’s Farm in Mexico, New York, these are the simple joys our family loves sharing with yours.

The Christmas season

A tradition,
year after year.

There’s nothing like a real tree to bring Christmas home—the fresh evergreen scent, the branches waiting for favorite ornaments, and the warm glow of lights. Whether you’re starting a new tradition or returning to one you’ve loved for years, we’re happy to help you find your perfect Christmas tree.

Explore Christmas trees ↗

Christmas wreaths

There’s something welcoming about a fresh evergreen wreath on the door. We make our wreaths and swags by hand at Granger’s Farm, in sizes that range from small and simple to big enough to dress up the barn door. The greens smell wonderful, and there’s plenty of variety to choose from. If you skip a real tree indoors, a wreath outside is a lovely way to give your home a Christmas welcome.

Warm up by the fire

Step inside the barn, where our handmade fresh evergreen wreaths decorate the room and rocking chairs for grown-ups and little ones sit by the fire. Our friendly staff will be happy to serve you a snack or a cup of hot cocoa from the farm kitchen. Take a seat, warm up, and stay a little while.

02 / At the farm

Autumn in the country

Good days.
Great pumpkins.

A pumpkin picked, laughter from the corn maze, and a baby goat nestled in your arms—autumn has a sweetness all its own. Watch the kids play, gather under the pavilion, and enjoy the crisp country air. At Granger’s Farm, there’s room to linger and spend a little more time together.

03 / At the farm

Life on the farm

Meet our
smaller neighbors.

With curious noses and plenty of personality, our Nigerian dwarf goats have a way of stealing hearts. Enjoy free baby goat snuggles during pumpkin season, or give our sweet mama goats a little love when you visit for your Christmas tree. They’re part of our farm family, and we’re happy to share a little of their sweetness with yours.
