Welcome to Granger’s Farm

A little closer to the land.
A little more time together.

Welcome to the official website of Granger’s Farm, also known as Granger’s Christmas Tree Farm, on Tubbs Road in Mexico, New York. We began with Christmas trees and grew to include pumpkins and Nigerian dwarf goats, bringing you something to enjoy each season.
