Visit the Farm / Christmas Trees

The Christmas season

A tradition to bring home.

Walk the rows, breathe in the winter air, and choose a tree to bring home. Christmas begins here at Granger’s Farm.

Find your Christmas tree

Walk the rows and choose a tree for your home at Granger’s Farm in Mexico, New York. Choose-and-cut trees are $65.

Prefer a pre-cut tree?

Our pre-cut Christmas trees are $10 per foot. Choose the size that suits your space and start your Christmas season here on the farm.

A little help along the way

We provide saws and sleds for your visit. Tree baling and drilling are included at no charge, so you can get your tree ready for the trip home.

Before you visit

Open Thanksgiving Day through Christmas Eve, every day from 10 AM–6 PM. Cash only.
