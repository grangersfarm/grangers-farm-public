Visit the Farm / Christmas Trees

The Christmas season

A tradition to bring home.

There’s a quiet joy in bringing home a real Christmas tree. Whether it’s your first visit or you’ve been coming for years, we’re glad to be part of your Christmas at Granger’s Farm.

Cut your own Christmas tree

Take your time exploring our Christmas tree fields. You’ll find evergreens of all shapes and sizes across the farm, with views worth stopping to take in. For many, the afternoon spent out in the fields is a favorite part of Christmas.

We recommend dressing for the weather and wearing appropriate footwear, as the fields can be snowy or muddy.

Choose-and-cut trees are $65. We provide saws and sleds, and baling and drilling are free.

A pre-cut tree for your home

Come see our selection of pre-cut Christmas trees. We have a variety of heights and shapes, so you can find one that feels right for your space. Trees are $10 per foot, with free baling and drilling.

A little help along the way

We provide saws and sleds for your visit. Tree baling and drilling are included at no charge, so you can get your tree ready for the trip home. If you forget to bring twine, we have some available.

The Christmas Tree Pickup System

  1. Grab a sled and saw, then find your tree. Pick up a sled and saw at the barn, then head out to find and cut your perfect Christmas tree. Battery-powered saws are welcome, and gas-powered saws are prohibited anywhere on the farm.
  2. Bring your tree back to the barn. Pull your tree on the sled to the baling room at the back of the barn, right where you started. We’ll tag it and give you a ticket with the matching serial number. Do not take your tree straight to your vehicle. Every tree must come back to the barn to be tagged, even if your vehicle is closer or you’re skipping baling and drilling.
  3. Head inside to pay. The cashier will stamp your ticket when you pay. While you’re inside, we’ll even up the cut at the base if needed, bale and drill your tree if you’d like, and move it to the pickup area. After all that work, it’s a good time to put your feet up by the fire with a cup of hot cocoa.
  4. Show your stamped ticket at pickup. When you’re ready to go, bring your stamped ticket to the pickup station. We’ll match the full serial number on your ticket to the tag on your tree. Please wait for the attendant to verify the match before taking your tree to your vehicle. Once we’ve verified the match, your tree is ready to go home. For insurance reasons, our staff are unable to help tie down your tree. Please secure it to your vehicle before you leave.

Don’t lose your ticket. Take your time enjoying the farm. Your tree will be waiting in the pickup area, and we’ll need your stamped ticket when you come back for it.

Good to know

A few useful details.

Which tree varieties are available?

Canaan fir is the tree we plant most at Granger’s Farm. Its soft, deep-green needles have silvery undersides that catch the light, and it has that fresh evergreen scent you look forward to at Christmas. The branches are sturdy enough for heavier keepsake ornaments, and the tree holds its needles well when kept watered.

Eastern white pine has long, soft blue-green needles that give it a feathery look. Its scent is mild, and its flexible branches are lovely with lightweight ornaments.

We also grow a smaller selection of other firs. Available varieties and sizes can vary throughout the season.

Can we cut our own tree?

Yes. Choose-and-cut trees are $65, and saws and sleds are provided.

What does a pre-cut tree cost?

Pre-cut Christmas trees are $10 per foot.

When are you open for Christmas trees?

Thanksgiving Day through Christmas Eve, every day from 10 AM–6 PM. Cash only.
