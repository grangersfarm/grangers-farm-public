What should we know before coming?

Please bring cash; checks are also accepted. There is no ATM on-site. Dress for the weather and wear sturdy footwear for walking around the farm, as the ground can be uneven or muddy. Check our seasonal hours here on the website, and follow our official Granger’s Farm Facebook page for weather-related updates.

Is the farm accessible for my visit?

Please call to discuss your access needs and current conditions. We can help you plan with the latest information.

Can we bring a pet?

Please contact the farm about the current pet policy before bringing an animal.

Where can we find seasonal updates?

Contact us directly or visit our official Facebook and Instagram pages. Our Instagram is @grangers_farm.
