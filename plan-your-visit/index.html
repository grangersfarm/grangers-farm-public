What should we know before coming?

Please bring cash; checks are also accepted. There is no ATM on-site. Dress for the weather and wear sturdy footwear for walking around the farm, as the ground can be uneven or muddy. Check our seasonal hours here on the website, and follow our official Granger’s Farm Facebook page for weather-related updates.

Is the farm accessible?

Wheelchair access is available to the goat area and kitchen. As an outdoor working farm, ground conditions can vary with the weather and season. We do not currently have a wheelchair-accessible restroom. For specific accessibility questions, please email or call us before your visit.

Can we bring a pet?

Yes! Friendly, well-behaved pets are welcome at Granger’s Farm, including inside the barn. Please keep your pet on a leash at all times and be considerate of other visitors and our farm animals.

Where can we find seasonal updates?

Find seasonal dates and hours here on our website. Follow our official Granger’s Farm Facebook page for weather-related changes, farm news and event announcements. Weather-dependent Flashlight Fridays are announced the Tuesday before each Friday.
