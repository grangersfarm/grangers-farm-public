Granger’s Farm is a multigenerational family farm in Mexico, New York. Family, the land, and the changing seasons shape what we do.

A family farm in Central New York

Our farm is on Tubbs Road in Mexico, in Oswego County. It is a place where country living and family traditions come together, season after season.

Life by the seasons

Christmas trees, pumpkins and autumn activities, and Nigerian dwarf goats each have a place here. Our visitor seasons follow the work and rhythm of the farm.

You’re welcome to get in touch

Whether you’re planning a seasonal outing or have a question about the goats, we’re glad to hear from you. Contact us before visiting so we can share the latest details.
