Visit the Farm / Pumpkins & Autumn

Autumn at the farm

Take the
country road.

Come spend a crisp fall day at Granger’s Farm. Across the road from the barn, take your time in the pumpkin patch and see what’s new in our famous corn maze. Back at the barn, stop to give our friendly goats a little love.

Pumpkins, three ways

Take a wagon into the pumpkin patch in the field across the road from the barn and pick your own pumpkin for $5. If you’re short on time, pick one off a pallet at the barn for $8.

Inside the barn, browse specialty pumpkins from extra small to extra large in green, yellow, brown, orange, white, pink, and striped varieties. Look for our largest pumpkins on display. Every pumpkin is $20 or less.

Corn stalks

Pick up corn stalks at the barn for $5 to decorate your porch for fall.

Our famous corn maze

You’ll find the corn maze in the field across the road from the barn. Follow the path past 20 to 25 displays that change each year, with moving figures and sound along the way.

$5 per person. Ages 2 and under are free.

Flashlight Fridays

Bring a flashlight and come in costume if you like. Head to the field across the road from the barn to walk the corn maze after dark or pick a pumpkin under the stars. You may see Michael Myers making his rounds. The displays feature moving figures and sound, with no jump scares.

Flashlight Fridays run from 7–9 PM, weather permitting. Check our official Granger’s Farm Facebook page the Tuesday before each event for an announcement.

Around the farm

Across the road from the barn, climb Mulch Mountain, go through its tunnel, or take a seat under the pavilion. On the barn side, stop by the playground, grab a snack from the farm kitchen, or visit our goats. For baby goat snuggle hours, see Plan Your Visit.

When is the pumpkin patch open?

What is the corn maze like?
