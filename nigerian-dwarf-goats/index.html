There’s usually a curious little face waiting to see who’s come by the goat pen. Our Nigerian dwarf goats are part of the family here at Granger’s Farm, and we’d love for you to stop and get to know them. Take your time saying hello. We still do, even after all these years.

Our Nigerian dwarf goats

Nigerian Dwarfs are miniature dairy goats with roots in West Africa. They come in all sorts of colors and markings, and even the grown-ups stay small.

Ours are big babies when it comes to attention. They love having company, so come say hello and stay for a scratch or two.

Baby Goat Snuggles & Visits

A little time with our baby goats is one of the sweetest parts of pumpkin season. Come enjoy free baby goat snuggles during your autumn visit.

Pumpkin season · Saturdays & Sundays
10 AM–6 PM
Columbus Day · 10 AM–6 PM

Baby goat holding may also be available on Fridays when staff are available. Baby goat snuggles end on the last day of pumpkin season, Sunday, October 24, 2027.

You can visit and pet our goats anytime the farm is open. Stop by to give them some love during pumpkin season, including Fridays, and say hello to our mama goats during Christmas tree season.

Feeding the goats? Please read and follow the posted signs at the goat pen before offering any feed. An adult must stay with and supervise children at the goat pen.

Baby goats for sale

We take our time choosing which goats to breed, paying close attention to health and looking forward to the different colors and markings each year brings. Before our babies go home, they spend the fall being held and snuggled by hundreds of children who visit the farm. All that attention helps them grow into some of the friendliest goats you’ll meet. We raise them to be loved, and it shows.

We raise one group of Nigerian dwarf babies each year and begin selling them in the fall. If you already have goats at home, you’re welcome to buy one. If these will be your first goats, we sell them in pairs so they’ll have company.

Babies can go home once they’re eating grain and hay on their own. The earliest goats are usually ready in late October, and typically all are ready by Thanksgiving, depending on their age.
